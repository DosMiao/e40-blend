      Formula

      Efinal = (ER·VR + EL·VL + EH·VH) ÷ (VR + VL + VH)

      R is the fuel already in the tank, L the low-ethanol fuel, H the high-ethanol fuel and T the target. E is ethanol percent by volume (E40 is 40) and V is volume.

      Both fuels, adding A in total:
      VH = [ET·(VR + A) − ER·VR − EL·A] ÷ (EH − EL), VL = A − VH

      Fuel 2 after pumping fuel 1:
      V2 = [(ET − ER)·VR + (ET − E1)·V1] ÷ (E2 − ET)

      Empty-tank ratio:
      VL : VH = (EH − ET) : (ET − EL)

      • The math runs at full precision. Results show 2 decimals and always round toward meeting the target: high-ethanol amounts round up, low-ethanol amounts round down, and blend percentages round down.
      • When the E85 content is uncertain, the plan uses the lowest value, so even the worst case meets the target.
      • Volumes simply add up. The small volume change when ethanol and gasoline mix is ignored.
      • Results are a guide only. Trust your ethanol sensor or a test kit for the real content.
